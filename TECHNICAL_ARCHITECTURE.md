# Technical architecture

- `scripts/sphere.gd`: direction/latitude-longitude conversion and great-circle tangent movement on a sphere of radius 250. The surface is smooth, not a terrain engine. Player is constrained to its analytic radius; there is no rigid-body gravity, terrain physics, jumping or slopes.
- `scripts/world_cells.gd`: partitions directions across six cube faces, 64 × 64 cells per face. Nine sampled neighboring cells are projected across face edges and deduplicated. This partitions visual props and discovery only: the planet is still a smooth sphere mesh, not cube-sphere terrain or a terrain LOD engine. Near face corners fewer than nine unique cells may be active. Seeded props are recreated when a cell reloads; they have no mutable state.
- `scripts/main.gd`: constructs the scene and UI, owns the world loop and streams those visual-only prop cells. The planet mesh, fixed enemies, herbs, outpost and three waypoints are not streamed.
- `scripts/world_map.gd` and `scripts/waypoints.gd`: a simple equirectangular diagram plots discovered cell centers and activated waypoints. Registrar/shrine interactions explicitly activate travel; the destination and departure must be clear of nearby enemies. The simulation does not pause while the map is open.
- `scripts/enemy.gd`: idle, chase, attack, return states with a tether to spawn. Range tests are spherical chord distances; they are not hitboxes, navigation meshes, or animation-driven combat.
- `scripts/game_state.gd`: two quest state machines, one-time rewards, XP, discovery, finite gathering, potion crafting, sword equipment and validation. `scripts/rank_rules.gd` holds explicit promotion requirements. There is no general data-driven quest/monster/item database.
- `scripts/save_store.gd`: version 3 JSON with in-memory version 1 and 2 migrations, temp-file rename for primary save, validates primary before backing it up, validates on load and falls back to the previous generation. Refuses to overwrite a parseable primary save with a newer version; an older backup may still load, but saving is blocked until the newer file is handled externally. Backup writing is not itself atomic; no checksum, migrations beyond versions 1 and 2, offline simulation, fsync, or encryption. Rename semantics must be verified on Android devices. Never claim stronger crash guarantees without platform tests.
- `scenes/AssetShowcase.tscn`: standalone source-asset showcase.

The simulation is local and there are no network dependencies. This foundation does not establish performance or suitability for a giant production world; coordinates, streaming, rendering, physics and save formats need redesign/extension and profiling before scaling.

Version-2 latitude/longitude cell IDs migrate by projecting each old cell center onto the new cube partition and deduplicating. The mapped display is approximate: multiple old cells may become one and future discovery XP may change. Original IDs are retained in `legacy_discovered`; promotion counts add a derived credit for old IDs that collapsed to the same cube cell, and XP, rank and other valid fields are preserved; waypoints start locked. Legacy IDs not matching the old coordinate format are retained but not plotted.
