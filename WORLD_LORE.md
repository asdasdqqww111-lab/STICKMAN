# WORLD LORE — MICRO WORLD: LIVING

## The Hollow Sky

The world is a **small sphere suspended inside a hollow sky**. There is no horizon to fall off:
walk far enough in any direction and you arrive where you started. Every child learns this by
running laps around the shrine ring at Havenbrook.

Long before the guilds, the **Cartographers of the Hollow Sky** placed marker-shrines across the
globe to measure a slow pulse travelling beneath the crust. Their maps are scattered now; the
shrines remain, still faintly warm, still measuring.

## The Pulse

Something under the world breathes. Once a generation the pulse strengthens, the dead rise in the
barrows, and the guilds recruit heavily. This is why a registrar in a village of 3 people is
patiently signing up new adventurers: the pulse is strengthening again, and the guild ledger is
short on names.

## Places

### Havenbrook
A ring of five shrine stones around a guild hall, a forge and a trading post, set in the meadow belt
near the equator at longitude 0.012°, latitude 0.010°. It exists because the shrines do: the village
grew where the pulse is easiest to hear.

### The Meadow Belt
Slime pastures, wolf runs and the resource fields that feed the village. Where every adventurer
learns that a heavy attack commits you for a full second.

### The Barrow of the Bone Warden
An excavated marker-shrine: an entry chamber of 3×3 tiles, a 5×4 hall, a 3×2 vault, and a boss hall.
Its guardian is **the Bone Warden**, a first-cycle Cartographer's construct, still doing its job.
It fights in three phases — *measuring* (70 %+ health), *resentful* (70–35 %), *unbound* (<35 %) —
and drops the Warden Greatsword, bone plate and an elixir.

### Shrines (fast travel)
Five markers: Havenbrook, the meadow stone, the forest stone, the crag stone, and the
**barrow mouth**. Touch one to light it. Lighting all five is a *D*-rank concern.

## Themes

- **Smallness is the point.** You can see the whole world; you still cannot save all of it.
- **Bureaucracy against apocalypse.** The end of the world is handled with forms, ranks and
  contract turn-ins.
- **Measured things become monsters.** The Bone Warden was a tool; now it is a boss.

## Naming conventions

Places: English compound nouns (*Havenbrook*, *Barrowmouth*). Monsters: descriptive
(*meadow slime*, *bone warden*). Items: material + form (*iron sword*, *frost staff*). Spells: element
+ motion (*ember bolt*, *glacial spike*, *mending light*). This keeps localisation into Arabic
straightforward — all strings live in the data files, not in scene text.
