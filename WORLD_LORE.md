# World lore

**Planned fiction, not implemented game content:** Long before the present guilds, the Cartographers of the Hollow Sky placed markers across the world to understand a mysterious pulse beneath its crust. Their maps are now fragmented. A young F-rank adventurer begins at a quiet outpost; the guild's small contracts may eventually lead to the history behind those markers. The nature of the pulse remains unknown.

The current outpost contract does not yet reveal this storyline. Continents, cultures, factions, named characters and narrative arcs remain to be designed and implemented.
